# Printing the reference coupon

## Geometry

- Nominal envelope: approximately 20 × 40 × 20 mm.
- Strut radii: approximately 0.82–1.22 mm.
- Mesh: watertight implicit union.
- Recommended orientation: compression axis vertical, matching the exported STL.

## Bambu A1 starting profile for TPU 95A

These are starting values, not a guaranteed process recipe:

- 0.4 mm nozzle.
- 0.16–0.20 mm layer height.
- Dry filament thoroughly before printing.
- Slow outer-wall and bridge speeds.
- At least two effective extrusion widths across the thinnest struts.
- No support material inside the lattice.
- Brim if the lower trigger layer shows adhesion instability.

Inspect the sliced preview for isolated one-line members, unsupported starts, accidental gap filling, and nozzle collisions. Measure the printed strut diameter before comparing the coupon with the model.

## Compression test setup

- Use rigid parallel platens.
- Record force and displacement directly from the machine.
- Test with and without a low-friction interface; platen friction changes the collapse mode.
- Establish zero displacement at first specimen contact, not at machine motion start.
- Use a slow, documented rate and record temperature.
- Include unloading if energy recovery and permanent set are required.
- Photograph the side view so collapse localization can be compared with the rendered equilibrium path.
